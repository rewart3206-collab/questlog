    Each quest you complete earns a ticket, up to 3 a day. A ticket gets you one run of five floors. Every floor you beat pays gold, and beating the dragon on floor 5 earns a free chest.

    Run in progress

      Your hero

      Level up your attributes with skill points to get stronger in the dungeon.

      How to fight

      • Roll and choose. Each turn you roll your dice. Tap a die, then tap an ability to use it. Each ability works once per turn.
      • Every number has a job. Big dice hit hard with the Sword. Low dice fit the Shield or a Potion. A 6 powers a Fireball, and a matching pair makes a Twin Strike.
      • Fix a bad roll. Split one die into two smaller ones each turn, or spend a reroll on all your unused dice.
      • Read the monster. You can see what it will hit you for, and each monster has a twist that changes which dice work.
