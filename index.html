        You get a new ticket every 4 hours and can hold up to 6. A ticket gets you one run of five floors. Every floor you beat pays gold, and beating the boss on floor 5 earns a free chest. Your first clear of each dungeon also earns its charm and opens the next dungeon. Each dungeon needs better gear than the last.

          Your hero

          Intelligence adds spell power. Everything else comes from the gear you find in chests.

          How to fight

          • Attack deals your weapon damage and can land a critical hit for double. Every attack charges your spell.
          • Spell fires once it's charged. Fireball hits hard and ignores armour. Poison Cloud hurts the monster every turn.
          • Shield blocks three quarters of the next hit, plus your shield's bonus.
          • Watch the monster. Every monster is building up to a big special attack. Keep an eye on it and raise your shield at the right moment.
